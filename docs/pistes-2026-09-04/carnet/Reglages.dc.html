Ce que ce choix écrit
Le texte exact ajouté à chaque génération de ce jeu.
CAMERA — this overrides everything above. The camera is directly overhead, looking straight down at the ground at 90 degrees. Not a side view, not a three-quarter view, not a front view. No horizon line, no perspective, no vanishing point. You are looking down onto the subject's upper surface: its top face is what fills the sprite, and nothing that faces forwards or sideways is visible. On a character, that means the top of the head and the shoulders, never the face, and the feet stay hidden under the body.
